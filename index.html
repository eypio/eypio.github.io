Homelab Rclone

A private backup workflow that connects its owner's home server to the owner's Google Drive account using rclone.

What it does

The application uses Google Drive authorization to upload, list, retrieve, and manage backup files in the account authorized by the owner.

The intended backup workflow encrypts backup contents locally with Restic before uploading them to Google Drive. This is not a public service and does not provide third-party accounts or access.

Privacy

Drive access is used only for owner-directed backup and recovery operations. This informational website has no sign-in form and does not request access to your Google account.

Read our Privacy Policy for details about access, storage, and revoking authorization.
